Wireless Setup

ESP32 Device Connection Guide

Follow these 3 simple wireless steps to pair your Air Quality Monitor with your Wi-Fi network.

01

Power ON Device

Slide the battery power switch on your monitor to ON.

If connecting to a new Wi-Fi, hold the BOOT button on the ESP32 board for 3 seconds to reset.
02

Join Setup Hotspot

On your laptop or phone, open your Wi-Fi settings and connect to:

AirQuality-Setup
No password required for the setup hotspot.
03

Configure Network

The setup portal opens automatically. If not, click the link below:

Open Setup Portal (192.168.4.1)
Select your home Wi-Fi, type your password, and click Save.
