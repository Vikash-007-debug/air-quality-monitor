Hardware Pairing Guide

ESP32 Device Connection Guide

Follow these 3 simple wireless steps to pair your Air Quality Monitor with your Wi-Fi network.

01

Power ON Device

Slide the physical battery power switch on your monitor to ON.

If switching to a new router, hold the built-in BOOT button on the ESP32 board for 3 seconds to erase saved Wi-Fi.
02

Join Setup Hotspot

On your laptop or mobile phone, open your Wi-Fi menu and connect to the temporary device network:

AirQuality-Setup
No password required for the setup hotspot.
03

Configure Network

The configuration portal opens automatically. If it does not, click the direct portal link below:

Open Setup Portal (192.168.4.1)
Select your home Wi-Fi name, type your password, and click Save. The ESP32 will permanently store it and connect!
Waiting for Device Connection Once the ESP32 connects to your Wi-Fi, it will instantly link here.
