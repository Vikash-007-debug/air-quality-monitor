Hardware Engineering

Industrial-Grade Sensor Suite

Every sensor is selected for low-noise precision, thermal stability, and immediate response to environmental anomalies.

PIN: GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6)

MQ-135 Air Quality Sensor

Electrochemical Semiconductor Sensor

Uses a micro-heated tin dioxide ($SnO_2$) sensing element. In clean air, conductivity is low. As hazardous gases increase, electrical resistance drops linearly, mapped directly through the ESP32's 12-bit ADC.

Target Gases: $CO_2$, Smoke, VOCs, $NH_3$, Alcohol
ADC Dynamic Range: 0 to 4095 ADC Levels
Preheat Operating Volts: 5.0V with Divider Protection
Emergency Tripwire: ≥ 1600 PPM (Automated Alert)
PIN: GPIO 4 (Digital 1-Wire)

DHT11 Precision Climate

Calibrated Digital Moisture & Thermistor

Features a calibrated digital signal output combining a precision NTC negative-temperature thermistor with a resistive-type humidity component. Communicates via a dedicated single-wire bi-directional digital bus.

Temperature Range: 0°C to 50°C (±2°C Accuracy)
Humidity Range: 20% to 90% RH (±5% Accuracy)
Protocol Bus: Single-Bus Digital Serial
Emergency Tripwire: ≥ 40.0°C or ≥ 88% Moisture
CORE: Xtensa LX6 @ 240MHz

ESP32 WROOM-32 IoT Core

Dual-Core 32-Bit Microprocessor

The central nervous system. Executes non-blocking FreeRTOS telemetry tasks, samples analog voltages, manages Wi-Fi reconnection, and performs SSL handshakes to Google Firebase Realtime Database.

Processing Core: Dual-Core 240MHz 32-Bit LX6
Cloud Connectivity: 2.4GHz 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi
Pairing System: WiFiManager Auto-Captive Portal
Hardware BOOT button: 3s Wi-Fi memory wipe
PIN: GPIO 35 (ADC Divider)

18650 Li-ion Autonomous Power

TP4056 Rechargeable Management

Engineered for untethered wireless deployment. Powered by a high-capacity 18650 Lithium-ion cell managed by a TP4056 CC/CV charging module with DW01A overcharge and over-discharge protection circuitry.

Cell Chemistry: 3.7V / 4.2V 18650 Li-ion
Charge Circuit: TP4056 with DW01A Protection
Telemetry Divider: 100kΩ / 100kΩ Precision Resistors
Live Cell Voltage: 3.20V (0%) to 4.20V (100%)
Pipeline Architecture

From Physical Air to Customer Action

Five interconnected architectural stages deliver end-to-end reliability from physical semiconductor reaction to live Gmail alert.

01

Edge Sensor Sampling

Every 3 seconds, the ESP32 samples DHT11 digital signals, MQ-135 analog voltage, and battery ADC divider without blocking operations.

02

Firebase SSL Relay

Data packets are formatted in JSON and pushed via encrypted SSL token authentication to Google Firebase Realtime Database in milliseconds.

03

Precision Vector Gauges

The web client updates mathematically calibrated SVG speedometers featuring cubic-bezier needle damping and centered display plates.

04

Live Waveform Analytics

Chart.js time-series plots stream the last 30 live readings in real-time, calculating instant Min, Max, and Rolling Averages.

05

Emergency Auto-Dispatch

If Gas ≥ 1600 PPM or Temp ≥ 40°C, the system trips the Black & Red emergency strobe, sounds an alarm siren, and sends alerts to Gmail via EmailJS.
